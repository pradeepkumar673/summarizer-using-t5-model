The mitochondria is generally considered the powerhouse of the cell, responsible for generating most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate (ATP).

This process, known as cellular respiration, involves a complex series of metabolic pathways that utilize oxygen to break down glucose and other nutrients.

Recent studies have also highlighted its role in signaling, cellular differentiation, and cell death, as well as maintaining control of the cell cycle and cell growth.
